THE PRODUCT / HOW IT WORKS

From a moment
to a usable record.

JobWarden is a private workday log for California hourly workers. It helps you capture what happened, keep supporting details together, and take your records with you.

It is not a job board, an employer timeclock, or a legal service. It is a place for your own account of your working day.

01 / CAPTURE THE MOMENT

Start with
what happened.

Choose the kind of event you want to record. JobWarden then asks for the relevant details, rather than making you complete the same long form every time.

Add the date, place, times, people involved, and a factual note. Attach photos of a timeclock, pay stub, or relevant message. Location capture is optional.

The interrupted-lunch shortcut helps you record that event quickly. The shift tracker can show meal-timing alerts while the app is open—not reliably after you close it.

02 / REVIEW YOUR RECORDS

Less scattered.
More connected.

Review your entries in one place. Filter related records, revisit the original context, and look at summaries of repeated events.

Each record keeps its original creation time, and edits are logged. A content fingerprint helps detect changes to stored information; it does not certify that an account is true.

California-specific information can highlight details to review. It does not decide whether your employer broke the law or calculate what you are owed.

Before you begin.

What is a PWA?

A progressive web app is a website you can install through a supported browser. JobWarden can appear on your home screen and open in its own app window. The install guide walks through the options for your device.

Does it work without a connection?

After the app has loaded and its offline files are saved, you can use it without a connection. Open it online once before relying on it offline.

Will my phone and laptop stay in sync?

No. Each browser and device has separate local records. Use export and restore to move a copy; there is no automatic cloud synchronization.

Are all my records encrypted?

The passphrase-locked backup option encrypts that exported file. This does not mean the app’s local records or ordinary exports are encrypted. A lost backup passphrase cannot be recovered.

Can I record a conversation?

JobWarden does not record audio. You can write a factual note about a conversation instead.

What happens if I make a mistake?

You can edit a record while preserving its original creation time and edit history. Deleted records can be restored from Deleted until you remove them permanently.

Can it tell me whether I have a legal claim?

No. JobWarden organizes your own account and offers general information. A qualified employment attorney or the appropriate agency can review your circumstances.
