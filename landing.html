02 / Process

Keep the details.
See the pattern.

  1. 01

    Log what happened

    Choose the issue. JobWarden asks only for the details that matter to that entry.

  2. 02

    Preserve the original

    Each saved record gets a fingerprint so later changes are visible in its history.

  3. 03

    Export when you decide

    Create a backup, spreadsheet, or printable summary. Nothing leaves the device by itself.

03 / Private by design

No account to open.
No server holding your story.

JobWarden stores your entries in this browser on this device. There is no sign-in, analytics service, or cloud record.

Local-only also means a lost or wiped phone can lose your entries. Export a backup regularly and keep it somewhere you control.
